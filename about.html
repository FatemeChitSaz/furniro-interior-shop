Our Story

Furniro started as a small family workshop with one simple idea: furniture should be both beautiful and honest — made from real materials, built to last, and priced fairly. Today we work with independent makers around the world to bring that same idea into modern homes.

Every piece in our catalogue is chosen for its craftsmanship, comfort, and timeless design — so your space feels like it was put together with care, not just filled with furniture.
